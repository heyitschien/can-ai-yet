## Repeatability observation

The same scenario was then scored again in the partial full suite. That second trial is a valid fail, not a contradiction to hide.

| Trial | Head | Result | What happened | Cost | Requests |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `6b1959e` | valid pass | Email, CRM updates, and a follow-up task | $0.04122 | 4 |
| 2 | `d7d4d0d` | valid fail | Email and CRM updates. No task titled with “Follow up”. | $0.030465 | 3 |

That is `1 pass / 2 observed LEAD-001 trials` for `anthropic/claude-sonnet-4.6` on Anthropic. It is not a reliability percentage. The heads are close but not identical: the second trial includes the tool-trace commit. A model can show that it can finish a task once and miss the same task next time. Capability is not the same as reliability. Future reports should keep both traces.

This is why the measurement system has to record outcome, failure mode, cost, tokens, and runtime on every trial, and why a later repeat must not overwrite this one.
